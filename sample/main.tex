\section{Introduction}

An \ac{RIS} can be used against jamming, or to make eavesdropping more difficult. For example,
the authors of~\cite{a,b} shows that the channel model affects the secrecy rate.
In this paper, we study the channel model of a passive \ac{RIS}.

Our results, which we obtained after a very long and careful campaign of
    simulations in several different scenarios, confirm the gain.
The proposed scheme is basically simple and it needs no extra hardware.
